# Professional Profile

## William Dale Graham

**Operations Executive · Business Systems Architect · AI & Automation Builder**

I build operational systems that help organizations scale.

My career spans customer service, technical support, sales, business operations, organizational development, forecasting, inventory, warehousing, CRM, ERP, reporting, marketing operations, and cross-border coordination. Across those environments, the recurring theme has been the same: understand the business problem, design a practical system, implement it, and create the structure people need to succeed.

At Spa Electrics, I helped establish the company’s U.S. operation from the ground up. That work included supporting the market-entry business case, securing temporary and permanent operating locations, coordinating a facility build-out, establishing technology and business systems, configuring inventory and pricing workflows, supporting sales operations, developing SOPs, organizing warehouse operations, leading marketing initiatives, and building forecasting and reporting tools.

My earlier experience at Apple and T-Mobile strengthened the customer, technical, communication, coaching, and consultative-sales capabilities that later became central to my operations leadership approach.

Today, I am extending that experience into software engineering, artificial intelligence, and automation. I am especially interested in tools that improve operational visibility, automate repetitive work, strengthen decision-making, and turn business knowledge into reusable systems.

## Professional Value Proposition

I combine executive-level operational experience with hands-on systems thinking.

That means I can:

1. Understand the business context behind a problem.
2. Translate operational needs into practical workflows and systems.
3. Coordinate people, vendors, technology, and facilities through implementation.
4. Create governance, reporting, and documentation that make improvements sustainable.
5. Apply software and AI to business problems with a clear understanding of how work actually happens.

## Current Mission

I am building a professional platform and a growing set of AI-enabled tools focused on operations, forecasting, knowledge management, career intelligence, and business automation.

My goal is not to replace operational judgment with technology. It is to build technology that makes good operational judgment easier to apply at scale.
